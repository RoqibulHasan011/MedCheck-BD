DGDA Bangladesh Drug Storage & Expiry Directive

According to the Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA), all medicines reaching within 60 days of their expiration date must be segregated or offered with clear notice. Medicines that have reached their expiration date must be removed from dispensing shelves immediately and returned to licensed pharmaceutical manufacturers.
