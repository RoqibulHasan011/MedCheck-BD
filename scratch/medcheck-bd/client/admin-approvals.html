DGDA Pharmacy Verification Protocol

Only pharmacies holding a valid Drug License issued under the Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA) and staffed by a registered 'A', 'B', or 'C' grade pharmacist are permitted to receive Verified Pharmacy status. Approved pharmacies will appear on public consumer searches and can dispense medicines with QR-verified digital receipts.
